Arcade survival defense

Skyscraper
Security:
Zombie Rain

Hold the streets or breach the tower.

In Lockdown, shoot falling infected and protect the civilians headed for the Rescue Grid. In Infiltrate, fight through the tower with limited ammo and decide when to take the lift. Extract before clearing enough infected and the district takes damage.

Play solo or team up in two-player online co-op. Share a private room code with a friend or browse public rooms. If either Defender dies, the run ends for both.
