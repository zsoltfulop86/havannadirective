Lockdown

Defend the district through escalating waves. Scores reflect waves reached, rescued civilians, eliminations, district integrity, and victory.

Infiltrate

Extract from each floor and take down the core. Eliminations, coins, tokens, and keepsakes contribute to your score. Faster completions break ties between equal victory scores.

Achievements

Open Achievements from the main menu to follow your progress. The full game has 32 achievements, and the demo has six. Steam syncs earned achievements to your account.

Legacy records

The studio website has retired its public death counter, achievement catalog, and callsign rankings. Visit the game privacy notice for details about current and earlier builds.
