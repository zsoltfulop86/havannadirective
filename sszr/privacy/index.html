Skyscraper Security: Zombie Rain

Game data
& privacy.

The game saves settings, progression, and earned achievements locally. Steam provides online co-op, achievement synchronization, and optional solo score uploads in the full game.

Controller

Havanna Directive Kft. (registered office: 1181 Budapest, Havanna utca 14. 7th floor 21., Hungary) controls the gameplay records described here. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to info@havannadirective.com.

Steam features

Valve processes Steam account and connection information for co-op, and syncs earned achievements to the signed-in Steam account. Co-op shows your Steam display name to other players. Public lobbies can be listed in the in-game browser.

In the full game, enabling Ranked Scores sends eligible solo results to Steam. Leaderboard entries display your Steam name, score, wave or floor, and Infiltrate completion time. Turning the setting off clears unsent results. Steam retains previously posted results. See Valve's privacy policy for Steam's handling of account data.

Earlier builds

Older releases may still attempt to send records when their Online Records setting is enabled. Death reports contain a random event ID, difficulty, build version, and UTC event time. Scores include the selected public callsign, random installation ID, difficulty, run statistics, outcome, and completion time. Achievement reports include the installation ID, achievement ID, build version, and unlock time.

Older builds used this data with player consent to publish worldwide totals, personal bests, and achievements. Turning off Online Records in those builds stops game uploads and clears their pending reports. Published legacy records are no longer displayed on this site.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides the legacy API, database, network delivery, rate limiting, and abuse protection. Cloudflare necessarily processes connection information such as an IP address while serving a request. Havanna Directive does not write IP addresses to the gameplay database.

GitHub Pages hosts this public website and may process ordinary web-server connection data when the page is requested.

Google Fonts serves the typefaces used by the game page and receives ordinary connection data when those font files are requested.

Retention

Individual death-event records are retained while needed for duplicate detection, service integrity, and abuse investigation. Legacy personal bests and achievements are handled separately from Steam records. Contact us about records associated with your callsign. Records may be removed earlier on a valid request. Aggregated death totals may be retained after individual event identifiers are deleted.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a portable copy of personal data associated with your callsign and public tag. You may also object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us using the address above. Because there is no account login, we may need the public tag or locally held installation identifier to locate the correct record.

You may lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information or your local supervisory authority.

Website storage

The Skyscraper Security: Zombie Rain pages do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The pages link to Steam and no longer request legacy gameplay records.
